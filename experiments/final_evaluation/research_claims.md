# RetailOps Research Claims & Evidence Matrix

This document provides a consolidated, evidence-grounded summary of the empirical and architectural claims evaluated across Steps 1 through 11 for the RetailOps decision orchestration framework.

Every claim is cross-referenced with its supporting experimental evidence, the exact evaluation jobs executed, the descriptive strength of evidence, and key methodological limitations.

## 2. Descriptive Evidence Level Definitions

To maintain rigorous scientific standards, claims are categorized using strictly descriptive evidence levels without numerical scoring:

- **`directly measured`**: The metric was computed directly from deterministic code execution, static source analysis, or logged execution outputs with zero extrapolation.
- **`experimentally demonstrated`**: The phenomenon was verified through controlled, repeated experimental interventions with quantitative pass/fail criteria (e.g., fault injection).
- **`implementation evidence`**: The property was demonstrated through structural code artifacts, interface compliance, and verified compilation/execution without modifying frozen components.
- **`simulation evidence`**: The behavior was observed within the discrete-event M5 inventory simulation environment under predefined scenario parameters and demand series.
- **`not established`**: The claim has not been proven or supported by repository evidence and is explicitly acknowledged as unsupported.
